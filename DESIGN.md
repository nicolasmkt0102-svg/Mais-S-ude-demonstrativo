## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an organic, warm, and restorative aesthetic tailored for health-conscious living and sustainable wellness in Cáceres-MT. Departing from clinical, hyper-restrictive diet branding, the visual direction embraces wholesome, relaxed vitality—centering on gentle weight management and genuine nourishment without guilt or radicalism.

The style combines **Organic Soft Minimalism** with warm artisanal touches:
- Generous breathable whitespace evoking calm and digestive ease.
- Softly contoured surfaces inspired by botanical curves and natural forms.
- Earthy, grounding tactile cues that signal reliability, freshness, and community proximity.
- Approachable, encouraging visual cadence designed to feel like entering a light-filled local wellness pantry.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around restorative botanical tones supported by rich earthy pigments:

- **Primary (`#4A7C59` - Verde Folha):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary conversion paths, active states, and structural headings. It provides deep organic contrast against pale backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#6B8F71` - Verde Sálvia):** Provides balancing accents, decorative badges, icon fills, and subheadings, infusing visual lightness and serenity.
- **Tertiary (`#C98B5E` - Terracota Suave):** Used sparingly for high-interest callouts, organic tags, discounts, nutrient badges, and warmth highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F0` - Creme):** Warm, linen-like surface base that eliminates harsh screen glare and creates a cozy, inviting environment.
- **Surface Variant / Cards (`#E8F1E4` - Verde Menta Claro):** Used for compartmentalized card containers, section alternations, and subtle interactive state foundations.
- **On-Surface / Text (`#2F3E34` - Verde-escuro Acinzentado):** High-legibility deep forest tone used for body copy and supporting meta text, avoiding stark pure blacks.
- **Direct Messaging Utility (`#2E7D32` & `#25D366`):** Dedicated to WhatsApp order actions, blended naturally with the organic palette to avoid abrasive visual disruption.

## Typography

The typography pairing relies on an editorial serif alongside a warm, geometric sans-serif to bridge human wellness guidance with effortless readability:

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Used across hero statements, section headers, and philosophical anchors. It introduces culinary credibility, care, and organic gentleness.
- **Functional Sans (DM Sans):** Selected for high legibility across nutrition profiles, step-by-step guidance, WhatsApp order triggers, and mobile viewports.
- Line heights remain comfortably open across mobile breakpoints to ensure unhurried reading and clarity for users skimming health benefits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, deploying fluid grid mechanics that expand gracefully into desktop viewports while preserving hand-friendly touch ergonomics:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Single-column stacked flow with a `1.25rem` outer canvas margin, ensuring that thumb taps and horizontal card scrolls feel continuous and comfortable.
- **Tablet / Phablet (640px - 1024px):** 6-column grid structure utilizing `1.5rem` gutters, allowing side-by-side product kits, recipe cards, and store highlights.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid capped at a maximum width of `1200px` centered within a `#FAF7F0` outer frame, with `2rem` gutters and generous vertical separation between narrative chapters.
- Section boundaries avoid rigid horizontal rules, relying instead on alternating pastel backgrounds (`#FAF7F0` to `#E8F1E4`) and organic wave or curved contours.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial dropshadows and heavy blur effects, relying entirely on natural illumination and tonal layering:

- **Layer Stacking:** The background base `#FAF7F0` serves as Tier 0. Floating content cards and nutritional highlights sit on Tier 1 using `#E8F1E4` or pure `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px border tints in `#6B8F71` at 15% opacity.
- **Ambient Herb Shadows:** Floating conversion triggers (such as the persistent WhatsApp floating action button or primary CTA) feature a soft, diffuse green-tinted shadow (`box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.18)`), creating physical presence without synthetic gloom.
- **Organic Cutouts:** Images and illustrations utilize soft clipping and organic mask geometry rather than sharp rectangular borders.

## Shapes

The geometric identity is defined by soft, welcoming curves that reflect leaves, river stones, and natural seeds:

- **Standard Containers (`0.5rem` - `rounded-md`):** Applied to form fields, localized tags, and ingredient tables.
- **Cards & Banners (`1rem` - `rounded-lg`):** Delivers approachable frames for client testimonials, natural product benefits, and store information.
- **Pill Accents (`9999px`):** Used on category chips, primary call-to-action buttons, and floating WhatsApp triggers to provide maximum tactile affordance and friendliness.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Direct WhatsApp & Purchase Orders):** Background `#4A7C59`, text `#FAF7F0`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), internal padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions gently to `#3D6649` with an elevated ambient botanical shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Learn More / Consult Catalog):** Transparent background, 1.5px border `#6B8F71`, text `#2F3E34`, hover background `#E8F1E4`.
- **Special Accent Button (Exclusive Kits / Tastings):** Background `#C98B5E`, text `#FFFFFF`, applied selectively to avoid visual exhaustion.

### Cards
- **Product & Benefit Cards:** Background `#E8F1E4` or `#FFFFFF` over `#FAF7F0`, with `rounded-lg` corners (`1rem`). Card padding is set to `1.5rem`. Outlines use a soft hairline border of `#4A7C59` at 10% opacity.
- **Testimonial / Nutrition Story Cards:** Subtle pale mint backdrop (`#E8F1E4`) featuring an accent quotation mark rendered in `#C98B5E` alongside the user’s journey summary.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Dietary / Category Badges (e.g., Sem Glúten, Zero Açúcar, Chás Digestivos):** Background `#FAF7F0` with border `#6B8F71` at 30% opacity, text `#2F3E34`, fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`), compact padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`. Selected state shifts background to `#6B8F71` with text `#FAF7F0`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Inquiry Fields:** Solid white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` border of `#6B8F71` (30% opacity), `rounded-md` corners (`0.5rem`), and interior padding of `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state features a crisp `2px` ring in `#4A7C59` with zero offset.

### WhatsApp Floating Conversion Hub
- Anchored to the bottom right of the viewport with a clearance of `1.25rem`. Rounded pill or circle carrying `#2E7D32` with an ambient aura shadow. Paired with a gentle microcopy bubble: *"Tire dúvidas com nossa equipe em Cáceres"*.

### Local Store & Trust Badges
- Cáceres-MT local harvest badges, store pickup flags, and delivery tags formatted with subtle terracotta (`#C98B5E`) icons and leaf markers, underscoring community warmth and direct human assistance.
